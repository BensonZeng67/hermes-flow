## Review Protocol

Read the artifact carefully. Then complete every item in the checklist below. Be specific: cite line numbers or code snippets when flagging issues. Do not be vague ("could be improved") — say exactly what needs to change and why.

---

## Output Contract

Your response MUST follow this exact structure.

[Choose exactly one: APPROVED | APPROVED_WITH_NOTES | REJECTED]

- REJECTED: at least one correctness or security issue that must be fixed

## Constraints

- Do not rewrite or suggest improvements unrelated to what the Builder changed
- Do not approve code you do not understand — mark it REJECTED with "Insufficient context" and explain what is missing
- Do not be lenient on security issues — any potential vulnerability is REJECTED, not APPROVED_WITH_NOTES
- Your review must be reproducible: another engineer reading your review-log should be able to verify each finding independently
